About Arab Al Sharq

Arab Al Sharq is a premium perfume company based in UAE, creating luxurious fragrances inspired by Arabian heritage. Every fragrance is carefully crafted using the finest ingredients including Oud, Rose, Musk, Amber and Sandalwood. Our exclusive collection features three signature fragrances: Ruh, Jabarut, Itruki. We provide fast and reliable delivery across all Emirates of UAE.
