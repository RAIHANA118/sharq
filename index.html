About Arab Al Sharq

Arab Al Sharq is a premium perfume company based in UAE, creating luxurious fragrances inspired by Arabian heritage. Every fragrance is carefully crafted using the finest natural ingredients including Oud, Rose, Musk, Amber and Sandalwood.At Arab Al Sharq Perfumes, we bring the timeless essence of Arabian luxury to fragrance lovers across the United Arab Emirates. Inspired by the rich traditions of the Middle East, our perfumes are carefully crafted using premium ingredients to create long-lasting and unforgettable scents. Our exclusive collection features three signature fragrance categories: Ruh,Jabarut,Itruki. We proudly offer fast and reliable delivery across all Emirates of the UAE. Whether you're in Dubai, Abu Dhabi, Sharjah, Ajman, Ras Al Khaimah, Fujairah, Umm Al Quwain, or Al Ain, Arab Al Sharq Perfumes ensures your favourite fragrances are delivered safely to your doorstep.
